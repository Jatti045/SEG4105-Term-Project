## Demo presentation

### Directions and expectations

1. Start with your project scope statement.
2. Address development methodology, architecture, and development platform.
3. Demonstrate your solution.
4. It is expected to demonstrate the source code that has delivered the requirements.

You may include any content relevant to your solution; however, you should focus on the following dimensions:

- originality
- depth
- clarity
- cohesiveness
- proficiency

**Duration:** 10 to 15-minute video.
